# Data Analysis, Preprocessing & Forecasting Experiments

This folder contains Jupyter Notebooks documenting the data analysis, preparation, and experimental work conducted during the development of the AI Kitchen Decision Support Proof of Concept.

The notebooks cover:

- **Data Exploration and Quality Assessment:** Examination of historical sales and ingredient datasets, including missing values, duplicates, data consistency, and validation.
- **Data Cleaning and Preprocessing:** Preparation and transformation of datasets for analysis and forecasting.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Investigation of historical sales patterns, product demand, and ingredient requirements.
- **Forecasting Model Comparison:** Experimental evaluation of Random Forest, Chronos-2, and TimeGPT using expanding-window validation.
- **Inventory Simulation:** Generation and validation of simulated inventory datasets for 2015 and 2016.
- **Proof of Concept Preparation:** Preparation of sales, recipe, and inventory information to support demand forecasting and kitchen decision-making.

These notebooks document the analytical methodology, experimental processes, and data preparation steps supporting the development of the application.
